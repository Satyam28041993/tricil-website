BOPP Film & Printed BOPP

Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene (BOPP) film is the industry-standard choice for flexible overwrap, high-speed form-fill-seal (FFS), and retail bundling applications. Engineered for optimal heat sealability, high tensile strength, and optical brilliance.
