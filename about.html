Who we are

An international standard, delivered locally.

Established in 2005, Tricil Packaging Pvt. Ltd. is a leading manufacturer of packaging pouches, BOPP films, printed cartons and laminated materials. Our quality systems meet ISO 9001:2015 standards, and every product is designed and engineered to suit your specific requirements.

Led by directors with more than three decades of experience in the packaging & printing industry, we offer the cost savings of an international provider while working with the responsiveness of a local company — with turnaround times among the fastest in the industry.
