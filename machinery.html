"08 COLOR ROTOGRAVURE PRINTING MACHINE"

A rotogravure printing machine is also known as a rotogravure printing press or can also be shortened to gravure printing machines or gravure printing press.

Rotogravure is capable of transferring more ink to the paper or plastic film than most other printing processes, it is noted for its remarkable density range (light to shadow) and hence is best for fine detail reproduction.etc.
