About Us

We Are Tricil Packaging!

At Tricil Packaging, we bring three decades of expertise to deliver high-quality, custom packaging solutions for food, beverage, pharma and FMCG brands. ISO 9001:2015, ISO 15378:2017 & cGMP certified, we combine precision printing with reliable turnarounds to create packaging that serves your needs — and stands out on the shelf.

Certified & Quality-Controlled Manufacturing

Custom Solutions for Every Brand

From design to execution, we tailor packaging to your exact requirements. Our state-of-the-art facility enables innovation, efficiency and scalability.

Protection That Performs

In today's fast-moving supply chains, high-performance packaging is critical. Without it, products quickly lose their freshness, quality and shelf appeal. Our multilayer laminates and BOPP films act as a barrier against contamination, moisture and damage. At Tricil Packaging, we help brands deliver products that stay protected, preserved and presentation-ready.

Protect

Shields products from contamination, moisture and physical damage throughout the supply chain.

Preserve

Keeps freshness, quality and appearance intact for longer shelf life and happier customers.
