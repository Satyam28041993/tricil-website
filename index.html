About Us

We Are Tricil Packaging!

At Tricil Packaging, we bring three decades of expertise to deliver high-quality, custom packaging solutions for food, beverage, pharma and FMCG brands. ISO 9001:2015, ISO 15378:2017 & cGMP certified, we combine precision printing with reliable turnarounds to create packaging that serves your needs — and stands out on the shelf.

Three Decades of Packaging Expertise

With years of industry experience, we deliver high-quality packaging for food, beverage, FMCG and pharma — ensuring precision and reliability.

Certified & Quality-Controlled Manufacturing

We hold ISO 9001:2015, ISO 15378:2017 and cGMP certifications, maintaining strict quality control at every stage of production.

Custom Solutions for Every Brand

From design to execution, we tailor packaging to your exact requirements. Our facility enables innovation, efficiency and scalability.

Protection That Performs

In today's fast-moving supply chains, high-performance packaging is critical. Without it, products lose freshness, quality and shelf appeal. Our multilayer laminates and BOPP films act as a barrier against contamination, moisture and damage — so your product arrives exactly as it left the line.
