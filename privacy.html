Privacy Policy.

How we handle and protect the information you share with us.

Last updated: 19 July 2026

Tricil Packaging Pvt. Ltd. ("Tricil", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit tricil.in (the "Website") or contact us. By using this Website, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you submit our contact / quote form, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, product of interest, and the message you send. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party profiling on this Website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the details you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, and communicate with you about our products and services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

3. How Your Data Is Sent

Our contact form opens your own email application with the details pre-filled, so your enquiry is sent directly to info@tricil.in from your email account. We receive only what you choose to send.

4. Cookies

This Website does not set tracking or advertising cookies. Only strictly necessary technical functionality required to display the pages is used. Your browser may cache static files (images, stylesheets) for performance; this does not identify you.

5. Third-Party Services

Some resources load from trusted third parties: Google Fonts (typography), Google Maps (embedded location map on our Contact page), and WhatsApp (chat button). When you interact with these, their own privacy policies apply. We do not control and are not responsible for third-party practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. Our Website is served over encrypted HTTPS. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Enquiry emails are retained only as long as needed to serve your request and to maintain our business records, after which they are deleted in the normal course of business.

8. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have shared with us. To make a request, email info@tricil.in. This policy is provided in line with the Indian Information Technology Act, 2000 (and its rules) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

9. Children's Privacy

This Website is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the Website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
